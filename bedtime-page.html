Pooh and the Very Quiet Night

Deep in the Hundred Acre Wood, the sky had turned the soft blue of a blanket, and Winnie-the-Pooh was looking for one last little smackerel of honey.

"Just a small one," he said to Piglet, "to help the stars come out." Piglet thought that was a very sensible idea.

So they shared the honey on the big log by the stream, and one by one the stars came out to listen. The water whispered, the trees swayed, and Pooh's eyes grew heavy.

"Goodnight, Piglet," he yawned. "Goodnight, Pooh," whispered Piglet. And the whole wood was quiet and warm and sleepy.
