🌙 Bedtime Story Maker

Pick a favourite movie or book and get a gentle bedtime version, read aloud.

Put it on a Yoto card

  1. Download the MP3 above.
  2. Open the Yoto app (or my.yotoplay.com) → Library → Make Your Own → create a new playlist.
  3. Add the MP3 as a track, give it a title and icon, then save.
  4. Tap Link to card and pop a blank Make Your Own card into your Yoto player.
